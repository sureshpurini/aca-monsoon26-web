2 · The area split

Divide 100 residual area units between compute and cache. Compute peak is α(A − m); achievable intensity is min(κ√m, I_max), so the supply limit is β·I(m).

Why the cap changes the conclusion, not just the number

Without a cap the optimum sits exactly where the two limits are equal: if one is above the other you are paying area for capability you cannot use, so you move area to the other side.

With a cap, intensity stops growing once the working set fits. Beyond that point extra cache buys nothing at all, so a whole range of designs score identically — equality is no longer required for an optimal split. The left endpoint uses the least cache among these tied designs, but all still allocate the same total area. Other workloads, power, or a different cost objective must decide between them.

Try dragging r toward zero. The model says "spend everything on SRAM" — and look at what happens to P while it says so.
